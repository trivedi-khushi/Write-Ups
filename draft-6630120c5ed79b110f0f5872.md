FAQ: if not experience, as a fresher? then make projects, do open souce and build in public.

FAQ: For projects try to give answers to the questions like: what did I make? How did I make that? & what is the impact??

1. Work on some personal projects- eg portfolio website, making your own apps of a desired interest, etc.
    
2. Contribute with your skills to open-source: Includes github, gitlab, etc.
    
3. Learn in public: This will show your initiative of #100daysofcode, #30daysofcloud, for example
    
4. other things you can do is start publishing your own blogppost, or create content on youtube, etc.
