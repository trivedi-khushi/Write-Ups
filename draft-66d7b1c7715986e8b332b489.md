## About Field Days

Before I write about my experiences, here's is a short section for someone who's entirely new to what exactly are GitHub Field Days.

### What are GitHub Field Days?

GFD focuses to include tech community leaders from all across the country as part of its "unconference" culture. Well, it's pretty fun to actually be a part of an event, where you have a room full of like-minded people, and the discussions are driven by attendees themselves.

### When do they announce about GFD?

GitHub Field Days are not conducted at any fixed time of the year. They usually release the registration forms minimum 1-2 months before the event date. However, its teasers & cities are generally declared 3 months from the actual event.

### Are they online/in-person to attend?

The very **first GitHub Field Day India** was conducted **online** in September, 2021. As announced on twitter, they covered more than 160+ student leaders from across India, to be a part. It was such a success, that [#GitHubFieldDayIndia](https://twitter.com/hashtag/GitHubFieldDayIndia?src=hashtag_click) went **trending** on Twitter/X 🤯

### Do they sponsor our travel?

It is absolutely free to attend GitHub Field Day. However, they reimburse the travel/accommodation cost upto a limited amount per attendee, after the event.

## Major takeaways from GitHub Field Day India Hyderabad 2024

This was my first GFD ever. And if you're someone who wishes to attend any Field Days further on, do take these **ice-breakers** seriously! I've personally connected with some amazing community leaders. And guess what, we're now together planning to collaborate for an upcoming hackathon!!

---

Moreover, there are actually **four breakout discussion rooms** that take place at a time. Each one consists of 4 different topics. These breakout rooms are conducted three times within the event (mentioned in agenda), where the attendees are given choices to opt their breakout discussion topics.

PS: Each breakout discussion has a 1-hour duration, and attendees are supposed to end the discussions with a white-board presentation. Also, you cannot repeat choosing one breakout topic.

---

**Fireside Chat** was my personal favorite of all. It included a lot of different miscellaneous questions & topics that you can actually get a whole new perspective about community building and effective leadership.

## What is star fielder?

Star Fielders are the student leaders who had carried the most constructive discussions and came-up with impressive conclusions as a result of the breakout discussions at GitHub Field Day. It's the badge you win after each breakout room is judged for their presentation.

### Did you win any?

Oh yes, I got 2 badges as a star fielder. Two of the breakout rooms that I was a part of-were selected. Each of the discussion members won the badge. SPEE DFTEST

![](https://cdn.hashnode.com/res/hashnode/image/upload/v1727364914447/0929af65-59c2-4233-9ba2-15f6bd57608d.png align="center")

### Can anyone win all 3 star fielder badges?

Yes, in-fact if you win all three of the star fielder badges, you can the GitHub plush toy, or either of the exclusive merch. PS: that depends on what the organizers have planned. I'm simply sharing the experience for the Hyderabad event of GFD.
