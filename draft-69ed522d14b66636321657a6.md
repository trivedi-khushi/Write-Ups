## What are Skills or Agent Skills?

Skills are a universal concept, and they can be used with any AI coding tool. They are modular, reusable packages of instructions to specialize in a specific task and extend AI-agent capabilities.

One way to opt for a smarter workflow is to use pre-built skills or build them yourself. That'll be worth noting the difference, even with the same AI assistant delivering quality-rich responses.
