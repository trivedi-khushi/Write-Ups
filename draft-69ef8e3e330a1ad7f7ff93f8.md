What you are describing is usually called a **Text Badge**, **Pill Label**, or a **Background Highlight**.

In web development, this is achieved by applying a `background-color` and `padding` directly to the text element (like a `<span>` or `<div>`), rather than an overlay that covers the whole image. Because the element is "inline" or "inline-block," the background only wraps around the letters themselves.
