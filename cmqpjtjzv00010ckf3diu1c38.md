## 1\. Chunking

— splitting documents into smaller pieces before ingestion. Size matters more than you think.

## 2\. Vector Database

— where embeddings live. It finds the most semantically similar content, fast.

## 3\. RAG

— Retrieval Augmented Generation. Teaching your model to look things up instead of hallucinating answers.

## 4\. Embeddings

— converting words into numbers so AI can measure semantic similarity. The math behind "meaning."

5\. Tokens

— not words. Chunks of characters. The atomic unit your LLM actually reads and pays for.

## 6\. AI Agent

— an LLM that plans, acts, and loops. Not just responds.

## 7\. Fine-tuning

— retraining a base model on your domain-specific data. Expensive but powerful.

## 8\. Temperature

— the creativity dial. Low = predictable. High = chaotic. Know when to use which.

## 9\. Context Window

— the model's working memory. Everything outside it? Gone.

## 10\. Hallucination

— when AI confabulates with full confidence. The most dangerous feature in prod.
