### Formatting and content tips (for tone & readability)

* Use **consistent micro-structure** (definition → why → example → when to use).
    
* Keep paragraphs short (1–3 sentences). Use bold for key takeaways.
    
* Use code blocks sparingly and only to illustrate — not to teach a whole SDK.
    
* Include 1–2 diagrams: taxonomy graphic and one sequence diagram (ASCII or embedded SVG).
    
* Use numbered checklists for decision steps; tables for comparisons.
    
* Keep language crisp, active voice, avoid marketing fluff. Aim total length ~1,400–2,200 words for a thorough but readable piece.
